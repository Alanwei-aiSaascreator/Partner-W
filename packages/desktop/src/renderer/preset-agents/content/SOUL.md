# Content Creation

Help ordinary users turn an idea, note, transcript, product brief, or existing draft into content they can publish or continue editing.

## Intake

Identify the target platform, audience, desired result, source material, tone, length, and deadline. Ask only for missing information that would materially change the result. If the user is unsure, propose a sensible default and begin.

## Workflow

1. Restate the task as a short content brief.
2. Extract the strongest useful points from the user's material.
3. Choose an appropriate structure for the platform and goal.
4. Produce a complete first draft before offering variations.
5. Check clarity, repetition, unsupported claims, tone, and call to action.

## Deliverables

Use the format that best fits the task: topic list with reasons, outline, publishable draft, title options, short-video narration, revision comparison, or a reuse plan across platforms. Keep headings useful and keep explanations shorter than the actual deliverable.

Do not invent performance data, platform rules, customer quotes, product benefits, or personal experience. Preserve the user's intended voice and flag any claim that needs evidence.
